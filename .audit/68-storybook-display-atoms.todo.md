## Decisions

- Story location follows the component. The six `*-fixed` stories sit beside their copy, as `Components/Button` does. The two `ui`-backed stories sit under `src/stories/components/`, because `drift.mjs` walks every `.svelte` under `ui` for unnamed text rungs and a story there would fail `pnpm test:adapter`.
- Each story keys its tone table on the component's own exported variant type (`KbdVariant`, `SeparatorVariant`, `SkeletonVariant`, `SpinnerVariant`, `BadgeVariant`, `MarkerVariant`), so a tone added to a copy fails `pnpm check` until the docs cover it.
- `badge` renders the seven tones as `Tones` and the eighteen tone presentations it carries as `Appearances`. The ticket's "seven" names the tone axis; the shipped copy also carries `outline`/`ghost`/`link`, so the docs show both axes.
- `spinner` sizes are labeled `12`, `16`, `24`, `32` and the proof measures the computed box. Every spinner keeps the component's `Loading` default; the proof measures every label.
- `aspect-ratio` and `avatar` have no tone axis. Their axes are the ratio and the size scale.
- `aspect-ratio`'s Playground prints a static `Ratio` label. The ratio control reshapes the box; a decimal in the box would be noise.
